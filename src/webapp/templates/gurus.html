What the value investors we follow held at the end of their latest quarter, from their 13F filings.

Aggregated portfolio
Each guru's holdings come from its 13F-HR filings with the SEC: the long positions in US-listed stocks, ETFs, options and convertible notes (the SEC's 13(f) securities) it managed at a quarter's end, filed up to 45 days after it. Short positions, cash, bonds and foreign listings aren't reported, so a 13F is part of a portfolio, as it stood up to four and a half months before. Each guru shows its latest quarter; its changes are against the quarter before, by shares, adjusted for splits.
